# Research discipline

- Do not claim an experiment ran without its raw inputs, execution record, outputs and report.
- Distinguish synthetic controls, exploratory model observations and preregistered comparisons.
- Treat identity/agency as operational research questions; do not infer subjective experience from text.
- Preserve failed runs. Use a fresh output directory; never rewrite historical evidence to make checks green.
- Buddie is independent upstream; update its full commit pin deliberately, do not fork it for local policy.
- File hashes bind bytes, not truth. Do not fabricate Claude transcripts for Codex run anchors.
- No credentials or private conversation exports in this public repository.
- Before publishing, run unittest discovery, a fresh E000 run, and replay committed evidence.
